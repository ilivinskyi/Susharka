# Privacy policy

Susharka does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **No network.** The app never connects to the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry,
  crash reporting or ads.
- **Your screenshots stay on your PC.** Captures are saved to a folder you choose (`Pictures\Susharka` by
  default). Settings are stored in your user profile. Nothing leaves your device.
- **Clipboard.** Images are copied to the clipboard only when you click a photo or take a screenshot with
  "Copy new screenshots to the clipboard" turned on. The app never reads your clipboard.
- **Folders it watches.** If "Hang Windows screenshots too" is on, the app watches `Pictures\Screenshots`
  for new images saved by Snipping Tool or Win + PrtScn. It doesn't look anywhere else.
- **Uninstalling** removes the app. Your screenshots stay in their folder until you delete them.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ilivinskyi/Susharka/issues.
