Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

In short: Susharka does not collect, store or send any personal data. It never connects to the internet, and everything it creates stays on your PC.

What the app collects

Nothing. Susharka has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or third-party services, and it makes no network connections.

Data stored on your PC

  • Screenshots are saved as image files in a folder you choose, Pictures\Susharka by default. You can open, move or delete them at any time.
  • Settings (shortcuts, preferences and the list of screenshots currently on the line) are stored in your Windows user profile.

This data is used only by the app on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Screen capture

The screen is captured only when you start a capture with the keyboard shortcut or the tray menu. The image is saved to your screenshots folder and is not sent anywhere.

Clipboard

Susharka puts an image on the clipboard when you click a screenshot, or after a capture if "Copy new screenshots to the clipboard" is turned on. It never reads the contents of your clipboard.

Folders the app watches

If "Hang Windows screenshots too" is turned on, Susharka watches your Pictures\Screenshots folder for new images saved by Snipping Tool or Win + PrtScn, and copies them into your screenshots folder. It does not look at any other folder.

Children

Susharka does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling removes the app. Your screenshots stay in their folder until you delete them.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts of its own. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact
